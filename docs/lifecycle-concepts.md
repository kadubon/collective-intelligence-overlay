# Lifecycle concepts

A capability is an exact candidate record for a tool or procedure. Evidence says
what an identified checker tested under a scope (task, contracts and environment).
A Decision belongs to a receiver and policy at a time. A receipt reports an
observed execution state. These are independent observations: generated !=
verified != reusable. A historical ACCEPT never grants present execution permission.

## Originals and projections

The Store remains the local authority for originals, invocations, allowances,
withdrawals and source checkpoints. Existing Overlay/OPA qualification and Executor
use-time checks retain authority. Lifecycle reports are disposable read projections,
not wire Records, a new DB, tokens or a global status machine.

Record references identify kind, issuer, ID and original payload digest. Original
DSSE payload bytes are verified before model decoding; reserialized view JSON has
a different digest. Old Decisions remain unsigned JSON projections. An explicit
export retains original signed payloads; original transport whitespace is not stored.
Historical signature origin and current compromised-key authority are distinct.

## Five types and uncertainty

`CapabilityLifecycleView` places candidate, evidence, receiver Decisions, execution,
owner observations, costs and withdrawal side by side. Capability alone does not
invent a generation event. A/B ACCEPT/REJECT is preserved with both scopes.

`ContributionObservation` records COPY, IMPORT, REUSE, FORMATION_INPUT and
NEW_CANDIDATE relations. Relations may coexist. Classification and declared inputs
are declarations; actual completed reuse has receipt evidence. Missing links remain
unresolved. Remote completion proves neither local installation nor quality.
Runtime dependencies, construction inputs and evidence support stay separate.
The same source delivered repeatedly remains one observation; two invocation IDs
with actual receipts remain two uses. Equal digests do not establish common origin;
different digests do not establish functional novelty.

`Residual` preserves a stable identity, target, source references, scope/time,
prevented inference and suggested next check. Unknown cost/causality, missing
evidence/authority, stale sources, unresolved effects and incompatible units are
typed. Original obligation strings and unmapped reasons remain verbatim. Null owner
or deadline stays unknown. Read-only reports do not close effects, retry, refund or
change a budget. Empty residuals establish no truth or completeness.

`GrowthObservation` compares independently retained current-assessment snapshots.
Receiver, scope, policy, finite universe, identity rule and admitted-entry unit must
match. Known accepted and unknown counts are separate. Exact endpoint set arithmetic
is available only for compatible complete local observations. Confirmed nonacceptance
and becoming unassessed differ; neither estimates functional growth. Period churn
and re-admission need complete matching owner-clock Decision history, including
expiry; endpoint net difference cannot reconstruct them. Period service/cost subsets
exclude records outside the declared period, universe or known scope. Wrong history
coordinates produce null service totals, not zero. Costs and throughput are separate
coordinates; source observations are never summed across receivers as ability counts.

`HandoffObservation` names Generate/Verify/Reuse/Account/Reallocate roles without
forcing a sequence. Proposed, received and assessed require their stated visible
basis. Received requires a local reception clock; assessed requires that receiver's
actual Decision. REUSE→ACCOUNT requires a completed reuse receipt. All residuals and
cost references survive export. This creates no transport, scheduler or authority.

## Coverage, costs and clocks

Coverage is complete/partial/unavailable/inconsistent within the declared local
selection, never world history. The Store bridge shares one committed prefix/anchor
across record and Decision pages; continuation preserves both cursors. Changed
related revisions are inconsistent; a restored generation rejects old cursors.
A whole-owner bounded page stays partial. A page ending does not resolve a missing
record. Explicit file material states its own coverage; declarations are not attestation.

Occurrence, local receipt, derivation and expiry clocks stay distinct. Cross-owner
material is not an atomic snapshot. Peer clock differences are not elapsed time.
No historical opening snapshot is reconstructed by applying today's policy.

Costs are referenced by original event plus cost position. Known subtotals separate
owner, category, unit and measured/estimated status. Unavailable quantities stay null.
Inclusive/unspecified wall durations are listed and never summed. Distinct physical
reports remain attribution-unresolved; no automatic reconciliation or currency conversion
occurs. Reservation is allowance, not measured consumption. See the
[reference](lifecycle-reference.md) for bounds and the [research archive](research-archive.md)
for what earlier observations did not establish.
