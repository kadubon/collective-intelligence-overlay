# Finite longitudinal accumulation study for 0.4.2

This source-only application measures future functional performance under a
declared synthetic task law. It does not measure universal intelligence, establish
an acceleration theorem, or treat stock counts as ability. Current execution and
publication status is maintained in [the audit](audit-042.md). Calibration and
confirmation are distinct cohorts; a positive result is not a release condition.

## Conditions and independent units

E constructs from scratch but receives no cognitive artifact across episodes.
M retains ordinary shared executable plans with basic testing, schema/version
matching and bounded relevance retrieval. C additionally authenticates original
independent evidence and uses current receiver-local CIO admission, FormationSession
and ordinary reuse. M and C can retain the same plan types, capacity and retrieval
size. All conditions use the same safety ledger, owner rights, primitive access,
independent checker and aggregate world/arm caps. M's ordinary quality checks do
not require inherited CIO PASS or formation lineage. Safety grants remain intact.

I is designed as checked private stock with the same combined experience and
resource caps, without peer exchange. A would compare locally adaptive allocation
against the same C stock/admission and total caps. The current fixed-allocation
driver does not implement A. Unperformed I/A panels leave H_SHARE/H_ADAPT unjudged.
The main confirmation comparison is C minus M, not adaptive minus static.

Two actual model proposers have different training experience: the producer
receives SQL tasks and the receiver receives numerical calibration tasks. Both
use the same exact installed Gemma and one owned loopback Ollama process. This
does not imply separate organizations, independent models or failure domains.
The independent statistical unit is the whole world, never a task, peer, model
call or checkpoint. Each world has separate owner DB roles, keys and CAS.

## Tasks and timeline

The SQL family requires a general read-only query with named parameters. Low
difficulty uses deduplication, local-to-UTC conversion, null/refund treatment and
grouping. Middle adds latest item rows, enabled customers and effective unit
conversions. High adds versioned rebates and join-fanout risks. SQLite's compiled
authorizer and progress handler operate only on bounded in-memory fixture tables;
the model cannot query an owner's operational PostgreSQL database.

The second family infers a degree-at-most-two instrument calibration from four
finite public observations. It must generalize to fresh values rather than return
literal outputs. Low has integer affine parameters, middle fractional quadratic
parameters and high less simple fractional parameters. Independent Decimal/Python
comparators do not execute the candidate's SQL or calibration procedure.

Six training episodes alternate SQL/calibration at low, middle and high strata.
Checkpoints 0, 3 and 6 use six unused parallel anchor forms with the same family/
difficulty mixture and online allowance. Model context is a fresh one-message
conversation each time. Evaluation does not mutate training stock or return
hidden answers, scores or evaluation conversations to training.

Final anchors use randomized full/empty/irrelevant views of the same checkpoint,
with the same problem, checker forms, model seed and online attempt cap. Empty
hides all learned cognitive procedures while preserving audit/safety history,
keys, ACLs and withdrawal information. The driver never invokes hidden old
bindings or sends them to the model. Retrieval only reads the explicit immutable
snapshot. The model has no tool path to a provider, filesystem or global cache.

Irrelevant plans are formed by real model requests under independently different
foreign latent contracts. They have original signatures, actual artifacts and
independent checks. Matching uses family, difficulty, revision, count and compact
cognitive byte size (one-half to twice the paired plan's size). Missing matches
are retained and marked unavailable; a training pool is never relabelled as a
placebo. Empty training stock does not yield an identifying stock intervention.

Prospective retrieval revision 2 applies the same family/contract/version and
eligibility filters to full and real foreign stock. The verifier reports actual
per-task retrieved type/count/byte doses, direct admission and each model context.
Missing, empty or unmatched retrieval doses leave the causal placebo contrast
unjudged, even when the whole stock pool met its declared size rule.

Separate challenges test an unseen reduction, schema/latent drift and a workload
with little reuse opportunity. The formation probe requires a new readings
connector combining SQL, calibration and group reduction. The model selects both
the executable parameters and operator order. A wrong order changes actual MAF
outputs. No complete composition plan is seeded or learned before this probe.
Full and empty snapshots receive the same extra proposal/check allowance.

## Blank receiver and resources

A fourth receiver begins with a new key, fresh DB and empty CAS. It receives
original artifact bytes and authenticated provenance, then constructs and
independently qualifies local bindings on separate qualification forms. Both
original proposer processes stop before the transfer probes. The probes use the
new local capabilities and a blank conversation; old provider access is absent.
Copying, import, local construction, qualification and maintenance costs remain
in the same world/arm budget. Successful import is not independent PASS.
This transfer panel concerns learned SQL/calibration plans. The separate newly
composed capability is checked on an alternate unseen form, but its transfer to
a new receiver is unmeasured. Model `uses` IDs are context references, not actual
executed dependency lineage; only the observed builder receipt proves formation
of reconstruction data for the installed factory.

The first calibration declaration uses two separate E/M worlds and an isolated
positive-control world. Each world/arm is bounded to 64 model requests, 655,360
conservatively charged input/output tokens, 4,096 driver RPCs, 4,096 reserved
execution invocations, 1,024 checker cases, 80 driver retrievals and 10,000 inclusive
seconds. Storage is bounded to 16 MiB CAS per owner and 128 KiB cognitive stock;
retrieval is at most three plans/10,000 bytes and a prompt at most 20,000 bytes.
Model concurrency is one. The cohort wall bound is 43,200 seconds.
The older counter did not separately reserve child model-side lookups (bounded
by the 64 model-attempt limit). It is not an observed complete retrieval total.
Prospective schema 2 reserves driver and child retrievals under one shared cap.

The exact local GGUF model is `gemma4:e4b`, digest
`dc35e8d9c6061baa6f0fa870975ab6932e2542b579b13ea0f199fa4bb7300c9c`.
Calibration requests use context 8,192, prediction cap 2,048, temperature 0.2,
top-p 0.95, top-k 64, thinking false, draft prediction zero, 240-second SDK
timeout and explicit per-task seeds. Weights and existing global server settings
are unchanged. No pull, cloud model, paid endpoint or saved-response fallback is
allowed. Binary/model/runtime/settings/hardware manifests are retained.

Intent is written before transport. Original request/response/partial stream,
usage, parsed data, selected parameters, actual CAS/binding, independent execution
and signed checker observations are retained. Missing final usage keeps the upper
reservation and is not reported as zero. A proven pre-send failure is distinct.
Whole-arm inclusive wall observations are not added to nested child durations.
The OS sampler begins before the owned server; polling can miss short-lived
descendants. Shared WSL PostgreSQL CPU, energy and GPU compute counters remain
unknown. API fee zero does not imply compute cost zero.

## Calibration, freezing and interpretation

Calibration examines E/M sensitivity (target 30–80%), isolated reference controls
(at least 90%), complete measured responses (at least 90%) and actual costs.
C-minus-M is not observed for selection. Low/middle/high results remain separate.
At most two calibration cohorts may be used. A floor/ceiling or invalid original
is retained; another protocol ID is required for a changed condition. There is
no confirmation inference before the final protocol/source/seeds/options/caps/
analysis/model and installed candidate-wheel identity are committed and pushed.

The primary endpoint is final full-stock world quality C minus M. Full-minus-empty
and full-minus-real-irrelevant interventions separately address H_ACC. Formation
success, restricted time and resources include failures and initial training
investment. A failed formation receives the declared 900-second restricted
endpoint; successful time is truncated at the same horizon. This is a finite
two-draft policy endpoint, not Kaplan–Meier estimation or a physical-stop deadline.
Actual wall consumption is retained separately, including values above the
restriction. Its prospective success budget is two drafts/20,480 reserved tokens
within the finite whole-arm execution/checker/time caps.
Bootstrap resamples whole paired worlds. Degenerate bootstrap intervals are
unavailable, with a conservative independent-world Hoeffding interval alongside.
Multiplicity is controlled across performed predeclared claims, and missing
panels receive no fabricated p-value. Quality MCID/cost margins and feasible N
are fixed after calibration and before confirmation. Small feasible N is declared
limited-power, without task-level inflation or claims of equivalence from p>0.05.

The complete second calibration used 205 real requests and 243,354 measured
tokens with no missing final usage. Both M stocks were empty; each final Q was
1/6. A post-pilot family review finds all SQL levels at floor, low calibration
at ceiling and the remaining calibration levels at floor. It preserves the
preregistered pooled low-level gate pass (0.5) separately and calls the assay
insensitive. No third calibration is allowed, and no C-M sign selected tasks.

The finite prospective confirmation declaration has three independent E/M/C
worlds in Latin-square orders, all existing strata and a final randomized
one/two-draft frontier on identical public/checker forms and first seeds.
Two training drafts are an unvalidated setting change declared before inference.
Equal world/arm caps are 96 model attempts/983,040 charged tokens, 8,192 driver
RPCs/8,192 reserved execution invocations, 2,048 checker cases and 256 combined
driver/child retrieval reservations. Nine-arm hard totals are 864 requests and
8,847,360 tokens; the fixed schedule can require at most 723 drafts. Cohort wall
is 15 hours, with a separate 300-second physical-cleanup grace. Five-second OS
sampling has at most 10,801 samples. Stock/context/CAS bounds remain unchanged.

Quality MCID and allowed harm are each five percentage points. At most eight
quality contrasts use 99.5% independent-world bounds (Bonferroni family budget)
and Holm-adjusted performed one-sided bounds. The 432-scenario planning exercise
uses shifts 0.05/0.10, baseline/correlation/censoring alternatives and whole-world
sampling. Three feasible worlds cannot guarantee five-point precision; the
distribution-free sufficient N for that halfwidth is 2,952 at 95%, or 4,794 at
99.5%. Parametric planning power is distinct from the bounded analysis, and
zero pilot SD never establishes future zero variance. Complete 20% compute
reduction remains unjudged without matched quality and complete costs.

Confirmation requires an exact installed noneditable native-tested wheel and
sdist, byte/import-origin checks in the driver and each actual child process,
the original trusted full-native gate and pushed preregistration. Runtime
comparison includes the binary, dependency versions, hardware, selected model/
show, explicit server settings and hashed relevant environment values. Original
transport/identity and offer-clock/accounting files are bound into existing CAS
and signed Events. Metadata-only runtime observations do not constitute a new
calibration or model-generation cohort.

## Observed confirmation result

Protocol `cio-042-accumulation-confirmation-v2` was pushed at
`ca4f7e412349f2068c32e26ac620883045a21742` before inference. Three new worlds
completed all nine E/M/C arms and 510 performance offerings. E used 181 model
calls, M 244 and C 244. Of 669 dispatched observations, 668 have complete measured
responses. The M drift response in world 619843 timed out; its 10,240-token upper
reservation is retained. Measured tokens total 805,556; charged tokens total
815,796. Total cohort elapsed time including physical cleanup is 34,897.687 seconds.

Final six-form quality is C 3/18, M 3/18 and E 2/18. C minus M is zero in every
world; its bootstrap interval is unavailable and the 99.5% independent-world
Hoeffding interval is [-1, 1]. That interval neither establishes equivalence
within the five-point margin nor excludes a meaningful benefit or harm.
Final full-minus-empty quality differences are also zero for both C and M.
Unlike the pilot, both retain nonempty learned state in all three worlds.
However, actual retrieved placebo doses are not matched on every offering;
the full-minus-irrelevant causal contrasts remain unjudged.

Every SQL stratum and middle/high calibration stratum is at floor; low
calibration is at ceiling for C/M. No family-by-difficulty band meets the
declared sensitivity rule. The oracle positive control remains calibration-only
(10/10); its absence from confirmation is not a new failed oracle observation.
The one/two-draft frontier preserves the same forms: C/M stay at Q=1/6;
E's descriptive mean changes from 1/9 to 1/6 at greater token consumption.
These repetitions do not increase independent N.

All 15 new-composition formation offerings fail, retaining their fixed restricted
time/resource endpoints and actual consumption separately. This does not measure
transfer of a newly formed composition. In the learned-procedure transfer panel,
original providers are physically absent and each receiver has fresh DB/CAS and
no training conversation. C/M full each pass 3/6, empty pass 2/6 and 1/6;
E full passes 0/6 and empty 3/6. The small descriptive counts do not establish a
population transfer effect, and the SQL forms remain at floor.

H_ACC, H_CIO and H_FORM are unjudged due to precision and/or intervention
limitations; H_SHARE and H_ADAPT are unjudged because I/A were not performed.
The observed lower token use of some full-stock evaluations does not establish
a complete 20% cost reduction or break-even: training, checking, transfer and
maintenance are retained, and energy/GPU/shared-service compute remain missing.
Whole-arm caps are equal, but E has fewer intervention offerings than C/M, so
aggregate E costs are not a matched primary efficiency endpoint.
